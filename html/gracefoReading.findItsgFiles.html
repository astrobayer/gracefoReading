gracefoReading.findItsgFiles

Locate ITSG product files in an archive by product and date.

Syntax

[files, report] = gracefoReading.findItsgFiles(archiveRoot, product)
[files, report] = gracefoReading.findItsgFiles(archiveRoot, product, ...
Name, Value)

Description

Searches an archive folder, recursively by default, for TU Graz (ITSG) product files named <satellite>_<product>_<date>.txt or .txt.gz, sorts them chronologically and reports the coverage including missing calendar days. With gracefoReading.readItsgProductMulti this turns a month of orbits into two lines. It is the ITSG counterpart of gracefoReading.findL1bFiles and behaves the same way, including returning an empty result rather than an error when nothing matches.

The product must be given exactly as it appears in the name. The release-1.0 density products are published as neutralDensity_ACC and neutralDensity_KIN, and those are the product names: one is derived from accelerometer data and the other from kinematic orbits, so "neutralDensity" deliberately matches neither.

Accuracy and Limitations

  • Only the file name is read. A file whose name does not follow the convention is skipped silently, which is what makes it safe to point this at a folder holding other products too.
  • A day present as both .txt and .txt.gz is reported twice, once per file. Nothing here decides which of the two to prefer, because either is readable and dropping one would be a guess about which is current.
  • missingDates counts calendar days, so a genuine gap in the published series is indistinguishable from a file you have not downloaded. It reports what is on disk, not what exists.

Examples

a month of kinematic orbits, with coverage

[files, report] = gracefoReading.findItsgFiles(root, "kinematicOrbit", ...
   'DateRange', [datetime(2020,1,1) datetime(2020,1,31)], ...
   'Satellite', "GRACEFO-1");
fprintf('%d file(s), %d day(s) missing\n', ...
   numel(files), numel(report.missingDates));

straight into a concatenated read

[t, pos, aux] = gracefoReading.readItsgProductMulti(files);
